--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Welcome to ChetnaSatra ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Information You Provide
    - Chat messages and conversations you create within the App
    - Content you generate using the AI-powered features
    - Preferences and settings you configure

2.2 Automatically Collected Information
    - Device information (device type, operating system version)
    - App usage statistics and performance data
    - Crash reports and error logs (for improving app stability)

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use the collected information to:

    • Provide, maintain, and improve our App's functionality
    • Process your chat conversations and generate AI-powered content
    • Personalize your experience within the App
    • Diagnose and fix technical issues
    • Enhance app performance and user experience

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. OFFLINE FUNCTIONALITY & LOCAL PROCESSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ChetnaSatra is designed with privacy in mind:

    • AI INFERENCE IS FULLY OFFLINE: All AI model inference runs entirely on 
      your device. Your chat conversations and data are processed locally 
      without being sent to external servers.

    • LOCAL DATA STORAGE: Your conversations and generated content are stored 
      locally on your device and are NOT transmitted to our servers.

    • INTERNET PERMISSION: The App requests internet permission solely for 
      downloading AI model files. Once downloaded, all inference operations 
      work completely offline without network connectivity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

    • With your explicit consent
    • To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests
    • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
    • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    • Chat conversations are stored locally on your device until you delete 
      them or uninstall the App.
    • You can clear your chat history at any time through the App's settings.
    • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

7. DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

    • All AI processing happens locally on your device
    • Data is not transmitted to external servers for processing

      communications (model downloads only)

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While 
we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
